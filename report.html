Methodology

Details on how each score is calculated · Per-car breakdown for each criteria is below

Reliability weight 19%

Two objective figures, matched to this exact listing: the TÜV Mängelquote for the car’s age band and the ADAC breakdowns per 1000 for its registration year (both lower = better). Each is mapped to 0–10 against a fixed market reference, then averaged 50/50. Older band/year ⇒ higher rate ⇒ lower score.

details
s = clamp(5.0 + 2.5·(mean − cell)/sd, 0, 10) per metric, then weighted tuv 0.5, adac 0.5. Reference (frozen, so ranking a subset never re-scales): TÜV mean 15.44 / sd 3.87; ADAC mean 13.0 / sd 8.9. ADAC cells are the official ADAC Pannenstatistik 2025 per-year values; TÜV cells are modelled from the official age-band averages (the per-model table is print-only). A model with no cell for one metric is scored on the other; with neither it gets the neutral fallback 5.0 and an n/a badge.

Condition weight 19%

Starts at 10 and subtracts what the seller discloses: accident status, extra owners, missing service history or TÜV, and defects mentioned in the listing notes.

details
Deductions: accident confirmed −4.0, accident-free not stated −1.0, −0.6 per owner beyond the first, no service history −1.5, no TÜV −1.0, −3.5 per disclosed major defect (leak, head gasket, rust-through, engine/gearbox damage), −0.4 per minor caution capped at −2.0. Clamped to 0–10. Caveat: this scores disclosure, not an inspection — a seller who honestly reports a fault is penalised while a silent one is not, and “accident-free not stated” is a paperwork gap, not evidence of damage. Defect severities come stated from the LLM findings (see Notes & findings), never guessed from keywords; an accident is charged exactly once — the declaration when the seller ticked it, the finding only when the tick-box says accident-free but the text admits damage.

Equipment weight 15%

70% weighted share of the wishlist actually fitted, plus 30% registration-year recency — a deliberate preference: a newer car's kit is newer-generation (infotainment, ADAS revisions) even with identical boxes ticked, so age is allowed to count here as well as in Reliability.

details
Wishlist: Roof / pano ×3, Adaptive cruise ×2, Standheizung ×2, Variable floor ×2, Blind spot ×1, Heated wheel ×1, Keyless ×1, Rear camera ×1, Towbar ×1. Towbar counts only when fitted, not merely “capable”. Year maps linearly 2016 → 0 … 2025 → 10, clamped. Options every candidate has (CarPlay, heated seats, cruise) are deliberately excluded — they are part of the search filter, so scoring them would shift every car by a constant instead of separating them.

Power weight 11%

From engine power: a base of 5.0 plus 0.06 per PS above 130, plus a bonus for a mid-size estate or a big engine. A proxy for relaxed high-speed cruising.

details
clamp(5.0 + 0.06·max(0, PS−130) + bonus, 0, 10). Bonus = the best of the segment match (insignia +1.0, mondeo +1.0, passat +1.0, superb +1.0, optima +0.8) or +0.5 for ≥ 1,700 cm³. A per-model entry in autobahn.map overrides the formula outright.

Cost weight 21%

One composite money score from three things a single €/yr figure cannot separate: what you pay (asking price), whether that price is fair (mobile.de's own verdict for this exact car), and what it costs to keep (the annual cash running cost). A cheap car can still be poor value; a well-priced car can still be expensive to run. Each contributes 30% / 40% / 30%.

details
Asking price — absolute curve, €10,000 → 10, €17,000 → 0, linear and clamped. Price rating — mobile.de's five buckets mapped to sehr guter Preis = 10 · guter Preis = 7.5 · fairer Preis = 5 · erhöhter Preis = 2.5 · hoher Preis = 0; an unrated listing scores 5 (neutral), since silence is not a bad price. Running cost (cash) — petrol, electricity, maintenance, insurance, road tax and HU per year, on its own absolute curve: €1,800 → 10, €3,600 → 0. Depreciation is displayed but not scored: it is estimated from the asking price, which already has its own third — scoring it too would charge the same euros twice. All three parts are absolute, so a car's Cost score never moves when you rank a subset. ADAC Autokosten structure over a 5-year hold at 6,000 km/yr at €1.85/l. Kfz-Steuer is computed exactly (§8 KraftStG); maintenance and insurance are estimates anchored to ADAC reference figures. Plug-in hybrids: the WLTP l/100km is a blended figure that assumes charging and ignores the electricity you buy, so it is discarded — instead 60% of the km run on grid power (18.0 kWh/100km at €0.35/kWh) and the rest on the engine at 6.8 l/100km. Full/mild hybrids are untouched.

Mileage weight 10%

Absolute odometer curve: 30,000 km → 10, 180,000 km → 0, linear and clamped.

details
Deliberately absolute rather than relative to the other candidates, so a car’s mileage score never changes just because you ranked a different subset — and a field of similar cars gets similar scores instead of being stretched across the whole range.

Location weight 5%

Absolute travel-time curve from Berlin to the dealer: ≤ 90 min → 10, ≥ 480 min → 0. At 90 min, Berlin and effectively all of Brandenburg score 10.

details
Travel time is fetched once per listing (carcache.py travel: Nominatim geocode + OSRM driving route) and stored on the record, so ranking never touches the network. Preference order is public transport → car time → car distance → straight-line; public transport is currently unavailable (the free DB endpoint returns 503), so listings carry real driving times. Unknown ⇒ fallback 5.0. The weight is deliberately small: visiting the car is a one-time trip, not a cost of ownership — and this criterion's wide spread means it punches above its nominal weight (see the stability section).
